# User prompt (verbatim)

"Create a mod called Clockwork Bottle. It should have an item called Clockwork Bottle. The item should store a second of charge every second ingame with a limit of lets say 60 hours. It should only gain charge while in the players inventory and the player is logged in. the charge should be showed in the items hovered tooltip. Right clicking in the main hand should grant the player night vision for 15 seconds at the cost of 15 seconds of charge. Each subsequent click should increase the night vision by the previous amount x2, and cost the equivalent charge. e.g. 15 > 30 > 60 > 120. If the item doesnt have enough charge, a message should display and no night vision given. Choose a simple recipe for me and a temp texture since Ill do that myself later."

Clarification: successful clicks SET the next duration, pay that full amount, and restart at 15 seconds after Night Vision expires. Failed clicks do not advance the sequence.

Implementation choices: non-stackable; main inventory/hotbar and offhand charge, armor/chests/dropped items do not. Tick-based game time, no offline catch-up. Per-bottle sequence belongs to its last user while Night Vision remains active; removal/expiry resets it (noticed on the next carried tick or use); another user starts at 15 seconds. Existing Night Vision is replaced on successful use. Vanilla potion duration remains paused while logged out; no wall-clock/world deadline overrides it. Recipe: glass bottle + clock + gold ingot, shapeless; vanilla experience-bottle placeholder texture. No external library required.
