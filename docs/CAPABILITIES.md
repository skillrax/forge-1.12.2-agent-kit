# Capability map

Since 0.5.0 recipient guidance lives in kit/docs/guides (index kit/docs/INDEX.md) and starters in kit/examples; "packaged X.md" below refers to the old reference of that name, now merged into the matching guide. Generators and the guide samples (items with behavior, block states and shapes, crops, tile entities, GUI container, entities with loot, events, config, enchantment, potion effect, commands) are compiled, linted and behavior-tested by qa/integration/Test-KitSamples.ps1; the packet sample is compiled and linted only (game tests cannot send packets).

Working examples demonstrate mechanisms, not every implementation. Similar recipes, materials, rates, tiers and appearances are reasonable extensions. A new mechanism needs one small proof when relevant. API availability alone is not runtime proof.

## Useful gaps

- Energy proof complete: original gameplay and both recipes user accepted. Evidence: `evidence/energy/1.1.0/summary.json`, its separate `user-acceptance.json`, and `evidence/energy/1.0.0/user-acceptance.json`. Pattern and lessons: `docs/ENERGY.md`.
- Fuel conversion and branching networks are established by the accepted RF network proof below; third-party adapters and large-network performance remain unproven.
- RF network proof accepted: user passed steps 1–5 and save/restart, then confirmed spawn residency matching the flight replay and closed the chunk concern under prior conditional acceptance. Pending-unload cancellation was independently reproduced and fixed. Source/lessons: `qa/fixtures/rf-network/README.md`; acceptance: `evidence/rf-network/1.0.1/user-acceptance.json`; automated patch and residency evidence alongside it. Recipient RF starter/reference extracted and checked from the candidate ZIP: `evidence/rf-network-kit/summary.json`. Focused adaptation accounting, held-port/dirty-marking safeguards, pending/actual unload and graphical controls passed. FE only; no third-party integration or large-network claim.
- Fluid and wrench proofs accepted: `evidence/fluids/1.0.0/user-acceptance.json`, `evidence/fluids/1.1.0/user-acceptance.json`. Guidance: packaged `fluids-and-pickup.md`; implementation/defaults: `qa/fixtures/fluids/README.md`.
- Item routing: user acceptance including multiplayer in `evidence/item-routing/1.0.0/user-acceptance.json`; patched routing and nine partial chunk unload/reload cases in `evidence/item-routing/1.0.1/summary.json`. Checklist, source and indirect chunk-loading lesson: `qa/fixtures/item-routing/README.md`. Actual modpack chunkloader integration and large-network performance are not claimed.
- Static-model recipient reuse: `static-models.md`, an original pyramid starter and a prepared Blender scene exporter are packaged under the Forge skill. Tool checks cover normalized geometry, UVs/normals and opaque/cutout mappings; source-format import/assembly still needs asset inspection. Evidence: `evidence/static-model-kit/summary.json`. The new sample has build/server evidence; graphical acceptance remains the accepted complex-model proof, not an independent sample gameplay campaign.
- Multiblocks, HUD overlays, input beyond ordinary keybindings and specialized AI: separate mechanisms to prove only when needed.

Thermal supplies feature ideas. Its full inventory, exact variant counts, large-network performance and third-party compatibility are not release requirements. Do not reopen accepted Workshop trials without a relevant change or reported defect.
